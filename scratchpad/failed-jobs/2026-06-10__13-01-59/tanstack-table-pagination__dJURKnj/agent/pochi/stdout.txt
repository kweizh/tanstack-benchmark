## How It Works

**Backend** (`server/index.js`):
- Generates 50 mock items with `id` (1–50), `name` ("Item 1"–"Item 50"), and `value` (100–5000)
- Exposes `GET /api/data?page=<1-indexed>&limit=<n>&sortBy=<column>&sortDesc=<true|false>`
- Returns `{ "data": [...], "totalCount": 50 }`
- Serves the built React app from `public/`

**Frontend** (`src/App.jsx`):
- Uses `useReactTable` with `manualPagination: true` and `manualSorting: true`
- Tracks `pagination` and `sorting` state, fetches from `/api/data` on change
- Displays `id`, `name`, and `value` columns
- "Previous" and "Next" buttons for pagination (10 items/page)
- Clicking the **Value** column header toggles sort direction (asc ⇅ desc) and fetches sorted data from the server
