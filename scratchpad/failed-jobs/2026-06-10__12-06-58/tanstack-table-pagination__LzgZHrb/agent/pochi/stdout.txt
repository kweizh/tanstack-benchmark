## How It Works

**Backend (`server.js`)**
- Express server on port **8321**
- Generates 50 mock items: `id` (1–50), `name` ("Item 1"–"Item 50"), `value` (100–5000)
- `GET /api/data?page=<n>&limit=<n>&sortBy=<col>&sortDesc=<bool>` — sorts & paginates server-side, returns `{ data: [...], totalCount: 50 }`
- Serves the Vite-built React app from `dist/`

**Frontend (`src/App.jsx`)**
- `useReactTable` with `manualPagination: true` and `manualSorting: true`
- `pagination` and `sorting` states are used as `useEffect` dependencies to re-fetch from the API on every change
- Columns: **ID**, **Name**, **Value** — all sortable (click header to toggle ▲/▼)
- **Previous** and **Next** buttons for pagination; buttons are disabled at boundaries
- Inline loading indicator while requests are in-flight
